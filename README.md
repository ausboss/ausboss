# AusBoss

I make ComfyUI nodes, workflows and LoRAs for image and video work.

- [ComfyUI-AusBoss](https://github.com/ausboss/ComfyUI-AusBoss): my node pack. Click a thing to mask it, crop, rotate and pad by dragging handles, inpaint and outpaint with your original pixels put back, upscale in tiles, trim video on a timeline. On ComfyUI-Manager and the [Comfy Registry](https://registry.comfy.org/nodes/ausboss-nodes).
- [Civitai](https://civitai.com/user/AusBoss): the workflows I build with those nodes, and the LoRAs I train.
- [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/ausboss): Qwen Image 2.1 LoRAs. Outpaint, outfit swap, consistent edits.

I also build tools that let AI agents work with real systems: [bluebubbles-relay](https://github.com/ausboss/bluebubbles-relay) and [agent-harness-resources](https://github.com/ausboss/agent-harness-resources).

A star on the repo helps other people find it. Tips: [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/ausboss).
